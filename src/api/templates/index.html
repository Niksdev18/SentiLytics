How It Works

From News to Trading Signal

Four steps from raw news article to an actionable market insight.

01
📡
Fetch News
NewsAPI pulls the latest financial articles every 2 hours across 10 major companies.
02
🧠
Analyze Sentiment
FinBERT + VADER hybrid analyzes each headline and classifies it as Positive, Negative, or Neutral.
03
📈
Fetch Stock Data
yFinance pulls real-time price data to measure the market's actual reaction to the news.
04
💡
Generate Signal
Smart logic combines sentiment + price movement to produce BUY / SELL / HOLD suggestions.
